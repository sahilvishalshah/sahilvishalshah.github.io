Airflow Through an F1-Style Drag Reduction System

CAD, CFD and a wind-tunnel prototype of a DRS wing.

  • CAD
  • SimScale CFD
  • Wind tunnel
  • Prototype

01The problem

A Drag Reduction System opens a flap on the rear wing to cut drag on straights. The trade-off is lost downforce. I wanted to quantify both.

02The approach

The wing was modeled in CAD, simulated in SimScale CFD, and then built as a prototype for a wind tunnel. Add: mesh, turbulence model, inlet velocity

03Results

At 250 km/h the simulation showed a 44% drag reduction and a 54% downforce drop with DRS open. Add: pressure and velocity contours, and how the wind-tunnel result compared
