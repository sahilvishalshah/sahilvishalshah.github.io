Enhancement of Performance of a Scramjet Engine Using CFD

Seven isolator heights, shock-boundary layer interactions, and one clear optimum.

  • ANSYS Fluent
  • ICEM CFD
  • Hypersonics

Supervisor: Dr. Senthilkumar P, VIT

01The problem

The isolator sits between the inlet and the combustor and has to hold the shock train without letting the pressure rise upstream. Its height changes both mass flow rate and pressure recovery.

02The approach

Meshing in ICEM CFD and solving in ANSYS Fluent, across seven isolator heights. For each I compared mass flow rate, mass-conservation error and pressure recovery. Add: Mach number, mesh count, turbulence model, boundary conditions

03Results

The 0.40" configuration gave the best balance of mass-conservation error and pressure recovery. Add: table of the seven heights and Mach/pressure contour images

04What came next

This dataset became the starting point for the Bayesian optimization project, where a surrogate model chooses the next height to simulate.
