Bayesian optimization of scramjet isolator geometry

Finding the best design in fewer simulations

A Gaussian Process surrogate guides CFD exploration of isolator height against pressure recovery and mass flow rate. On an existing 16-trial CFD dataset, the method finds the best design in far fewer runs than a full manual sweep. The model now recommends the most informative next height to simulate in ANSYS.

  • Bayesian optimization
  • Gaussian Process
  • ANSYS Fluent
  • Python

Supervisor: Dr. Prashik Sunil Gaikwad, VIT

5-DOF robotic arm: design, modeling and control

Simulated step responses checked against real hardware

Kinematics from Denavit-Hartenberg parameters, dynamics from the Lagrangian, linearized into a state-space model for pole-placement control. Independent-joint PID controllers tuned in MATLAB/Simulink are benchmarked against it. The physical build with servo motors and a microcontroller exposes friction and backlash the model leaves out.

  • MATLAB / Simulink
  • Denavit-Hartenberg
  • State-space
  • PID
  • Servo + microcontroller

Supervisor: Dr. Mandala Chinna Obaiah, VIT

Heat transfer from a gun barrel

Which fin shape cools a 5.56mm barrel best?

A comparative FEA study of V, L, Y and T fin configurations on a 5.56mm NATO-caliber barrel. Stress singularities at the fin roots led me to write an ANSYS Fluent UDF for a periodic mass-flow inlet that captures transient heat flux. Co-authored with Pratham Vashistha, targeting a Q1/Q2 journal.

  • ANSYS Fluent
  • FEA
  • UDF (C)
  • Thermal-structural

Supervisor: Dr. Rajesh Kanna, VIT

Scramjet engine performance with CFD

Seven isolator heights, one clear optimum

ANSYS Fluent study of shock-boundary layer interactions across seven isolator heights and their effect on mass flow rate and pressure recovery. The 0.40" configuration gave the best balance of mass-conservation error and pressure recovery. The dataset now feeds the Bayesian optimization project above.
