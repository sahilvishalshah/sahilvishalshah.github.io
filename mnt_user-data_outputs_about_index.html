Get in touch

I test engineering ideas in simulation before they touch hardware.

Most of my work starts with a question a prototype would answer slowly and expensively: which isolator height recovers the most pressure, which fin shape sheds the most heat. I model it, check where the model breaks, and refine it until the numbers can be trusted.

I also like seeing the result on real hardware. The 5-DOF arm compares simulated step responses against a physical build, and the DRS project took a CFD result into a wind tunnel. As captain of Team Vyadh, I coordinate mechanical, electronics and software so a rover works as one system.
